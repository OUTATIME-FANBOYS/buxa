Problem: the same concept, the camp's zone and the signed-in actor, is resolved three ways. One throws, one returns null, and the zone is read from the database on each call.

Solution: one camp context module resolves the actor and zone once per request and passes the result to services.

  • Locality: a change to zone rules lands in one place
  • Leverage: one resolver, every composer calls it
  • Tests: one interface, no stubbing of orgs.get in each service
  • Repeated reads of the camp row go away with it

Top recommendation

Deepen the camp context

It is strong, in-process, and conflicts with no ADR. The same zone and actor are resolved three ways today, and the camp row is read again on most requests. One resolver removes the duplication and most of the repeated reads. Start here, then take the domain rules (candidate C) next.

Separate from this review: the member-rename audit row and the owner-only archive grant are small fixes, and they should not wait for any refactor.
